Charles Duhigg tells the household version in *The Power of Habit* (2012): an odor-eliminating spray that flopped until the company realized habituation was the problem, then sold the bottle as the reward at the end of a cleaning habit.

This package is that move for writing. Make the smell visible, then take it off. The original tale is Duhigg's. Read him.

Written **aiBreze**. npm **`aibreze`**. Domain **aibreze.com**. A spray, not a breeze.
